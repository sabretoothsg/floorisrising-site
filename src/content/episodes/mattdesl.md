This is an 6 episode series on generative artists who we found interesting. 6 artists in total who we found contributes something unique in the current generative art landscape, they span different blockchains/platforms/aesthetics/personal histories.

Show notes;

-   Matt's drops Subscapes on Artblocks, but also drops companion pieces on HEN at the same time, one of the first instances of multi-platform drops.
-   How Matt is a new breed of coders who come into NFTs not from a "maxi" tech background but more platform/chain agnostic.
